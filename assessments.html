Therapeutic actionability assessments

The UK Therapeutic Actionability Hub provides a structured process for assessing potential therapeutic opportunities for individuals living with rare genetic conditions.

How to submit a case

Cases are submitted through our secure online submission form. The form collects the information required by the Hub to undertake an initial assessment of the case.

Please provide as much relevant clinical and genetic information as possible. Complete information helps us assess the case efficiently and identify the appropriate expertise.

Submit your case

How we assess cases

Each submission is reviewed to evaluate the potential therapeutic actionability of the genetic finding. The assessment considers the relationship between the genetic variant and disease mechanism, the available scientific and clinical evidence, and potential therapeutic approaches.

Depending on the case, this may include consideration of existing medicines, drug repurposing opportunities, emerging molecular or genetic therapies, and individualised therapeutic approaches.

Relevant clinical, genomic, computational and therapeutic expertise can be brought together to evaluate the evidence and identify appropriate next steps.

What happens after an assessment?

Following assessment, we will provide feedback summarising the outcome of the review and, where appropriate, potential next steps.

An assessment does not necessarily mean that a therapeutic option will be identified. In some cases, the available evidence may not currently support a therapeutic approach. Where a potentially actionable avenue is identified, further investigation or discussion with relevant experts may be recommended.

Important information

The Hub's assessment is intended to support evaluation of potential therapeutic opportunities. It does not replace clinical assessment, medical advice or decision-making by the clinical team responsible for an individual's care.
