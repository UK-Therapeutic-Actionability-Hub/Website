What is the UK Therapeutic Actionability Hub?

The UK Therapeutic Actionability Hub is a collaborative platform bringing together expertise to assess whether individual genetic cases may have potential therapeutic options.

Our aim

We aim to support clinicians, patients and researchers by providing a structured route for evaluating therapeutic actionability and identifying appropriate next steps.

A collaborative approach

The Hub connects clinical, genomic, computational and therapeutic expertise across participating organisations. By bringing these perspectives together, we seek to make assessment more systematic, transparent and accessible.
