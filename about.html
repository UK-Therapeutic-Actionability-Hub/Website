What is the UK Therapeutic Actionability Hub?

The UK Therapeutic Actionability Hub is a nationwide initiative to standardise, systematise, and align actionability assessments for individuals living with rare genetic conditions.

Our aim

We aim to support clinicians, patients and researchers by providing a structured route for evaluating therapeutic actionability and identifying appropriate next steps for individuals living with rare genetic conditions.

Advances in genomic medicine are enabling an increasing number of people living with rare genetic conditions to receive a molecular diagnosis. For some of these conditions, emerging therapeutic approaches may offer opportunities for treatment. Identifying and evaluating these opportunities, however, can require expertise spanning clinical genetics, genomics, molecular biology, computational analysis and therapeutic development.

Our partners are bringing this expertise together to assess the therapeutic actionability of individual cases.

A collaborative approach

We aim to support clinicians, patients and researchers by providing a structured route for evaluating therapeutic actionability and identifying appropriate next steps for individuals living with rare genetic conditions.

Our goal is to help determine whether there is sufficient evidence to justify further exploration of a potential treatment strategy, and to connect clinicians and families to colleagues worldwide where appropriate.

What do we mean by therapeutic actionability?

Therapeutic actionability describes the potential for a genetic finding to inform a treatment strategy. This may include existing medicines, clinical trials or genetic therapies including highly individualised therapeutic approaches.

Not every genetic diagnosis will currently be therapeutically actionable. Assessment therefore requires careful consideration of the underlying disease mechanism, the specific genetic variant, available experimental and clinical evidence, and the feasibility of potential therapeutic approaches.

Who can submit a case?

Cases can be submitted for consideration where there is a confirmed genetic diagnosis.

We allow patients, families, clinicians, researchers, patient organisations etc. to submit a case. While we are mainly focused on UK cases, we will also assess non-UK cases.
